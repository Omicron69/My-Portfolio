03 / About

A bit about me

I'm a Computer Science graduate First-Class Honours now specialising in machine learning and computer vision through an MSc in Artificial Intelligence at Kingston University London. I pair mathematical precision with human-centred design, translating complex algorithms into intuitive, enjoyable web applications.

Recent work spans real-time browser gesture recognition that powers a Duolingo-style game for learning British Sign Language fingerspelling (bslgame.co.uk), and front-end leadership on stakeholder-focused healthcare data platforms. I care about making data pipelines leak-free, scalable, and accessible. My repositories and live deployments are at github.com/Omicron69.

Away from the terminal, I'm endlessly curious about how people live, eat, and communicate. Growing up fluent in English, Bengali, Hindi, and Urdu, plus conversational Japanese, shaped how I listen and connect. Fast-paced hospitality kitchens and coordinating events for three thousand attendees taught me composure and empathy under pressure, and I bring both to engineering teams. Whether I'm training neural networks or studying retention metrics to grow my YouTube channel past two million views, I'm always looking for the person on the other side of the screen.
